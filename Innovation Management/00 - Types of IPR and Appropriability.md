# Why Intellectual Property Rights matter
**Core problem:** IPRs prevent other people from copying your idea and benefitting from it.

### The Matrix
Economists classify goods along two dimensions: rivalry and excludability.
#### Rivalry
If I use this goods you cannot use it, and the owner can make you pay for it.
- Rivalry good: Coffe
- Non-Rivalry good: a theorem
#### Excludability
If the owner can stop others who does not pay from using it.
- Excludability good: Netflix
- Non-Excludability good: a theorem

From this two we build a matrix

### Knowledge as Public good
- **Non-rival:** once produced the knowledge can be used by anyone at almost zero cost
- **Hard to exclude:** once disclosed ideas travel
Even though, using knowledge requires skill.

## The Appropriability Problem
**Appropriability:** the ability of the innovator to reap the economic benefits of its investments in innovation by limiting imitation by rivals.
